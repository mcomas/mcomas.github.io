Marc Comas-Cufí

Associate professor in Statistics

Universitat de Girona

I am an Associate Professor in Statistics at the University of Girona and a member of the Statistics and Compositional Data Analysis research group. My work connects statistical methodology with applied collaborations in health, epidemiology, environmental data, and clinical research.

My research interests include compositional data analysis, multivariate count data, clustering methods, survival analysis, competing risks, causal inference, and statistical computing. I am especially interested in methods that remain useful in real applied workflows, where data are complex, imperfect, and often collected for purposes other than research.

I hold a PhD in Compositional Data Analysis from the University of Girona, with earlier training in mathematics, financial engineering, computer science, and secondary education. This background shapes a practical approach to statistics: methodologically careful, computationally grounded, and attentive to how models are used in teaching, research, and applied collaboration.

I work mainly with R and Python for statistical analysis and data management, and I use open-source tools such as RStudio, GNU Make, and Git for reproducible research and collaboration.

Research Areas

My research combines statistical methodology, health data analysis, and scientific computing. Current lines of work include:

  • Compositional and multivariate count data. Models and transformations for data constrained by totals, proportions, or component-wise dependence.
  • Health data and epidemiology. Applied work with electronic health records, cardiovascular risk, neurointervention, respiratory outcomes, dementia, and environmental exposures.
  • Survival analysis, competing risks, and causal inference. Methods for longitudinal observational data, treatment effects, event-time outcomes, and risk prediction.
  • Statistical computing. Reproducible workflows, data pipelines, simulation studies, and software-oriented statistical practice.
